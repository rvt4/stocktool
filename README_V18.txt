StockTool V18 — Institutional Business-Aware Patch

WHAT CHANGED
1. Lifecycle classification now uses a Growth Persistence / Compounding Potential score.
   It blends 3-, 5- and 10-year growth, consensus growth, ROIC, FCF consistency,
   margin stability, pricing power, moat, volatility and company scale.
   Stable consumer staples can no longer become Hyper Growth from a short pricing or
   acquisition spike; KDP-like businesses resolve to Compounder/Dividend Compounder.

2. Premium persistence is industry-aware and economics-aware.
   Software, healthcare innovation and durable consumer brands retain more justified
   premium than commodity, banking or cyclical businesses. High-margin, fast-growing
   consumer brands receive a systematic emerging-brand bonus—no ticker overrides.

3. Valuation method weights are business-aware.
   Scaling brands and innovation businesses receive more weight on forward revenue,
   earnings and DCF methods. Mature/capital-intensive businesses receive more weight
   on cash flow, owner earnings and EV/EBITDA. Weak or unsuitable methods remain
   down-weighted with diagnostics.

4. Investment Committee score is quality-first.
   Business quality is the largest component, followed by expected return, confidence,
   moat, pricing power, compounding potential, margin of safety, downside protection,
   capital allocation and balance sheet strength.

5. New diagnostics include:
   - lifecycle.compoundingPotential
   - lifecycle.growthPersistenceScore
   - lifecycle.persistenceGrowth
   - premiumPersistenceModel.industryAnchor
   - premiumPersistenceModel.consumerBrandBonus
   - methodSelection business-aware flags
