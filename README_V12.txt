StockTool V12 — Premium Persistence & Return Transparency

Changes:
- Added a premium-persistence-aware exit-multiple model.
- Exit multiples now use a smoothed late-stage valuation growth signal rather than only the final forecast year's growth.
- Durable businesses can retain a modest portion of their sector/company premium when moat, forecast reliability, and premium persistence support it.
- The discipline layer now exposes premium floors and effective valuation growth in the audit output.
- Added Fundamental CAGR and Valuation Impact to the ticker summary box.
- Renamed Expected CAGR in the detail view to Expected Investor CAGR to distinguish business compounding from shareholder return.

This update is intentionally selective: it protects durable growth/compounder businesses from excessive compression without restoring the extreme valuations seen before V10/V11.
